A router-management system I built solo to replace per-device commercial licensing, then extended into full vehicle telematics. More than 2,000 vehicles and devices are under management.

  • Device control plane. Outbound MQTT telemetry, JSON-RPC/UCI control and OTA firmware rollouts across 2,000+ devices.

  • Time-series storage. TimescaleDB over PostgreSQL, sized for a fleet that is always moving and often offline.
  • Compliance vault. Automated PrDP, NLTAA and roadworthy expiry tracking, with workflow automation and reporting.

Reconciled to the general ledger

Ledger and money-movement architecture for a South African retail wallet. Pilot live with Big Save for university students in Pretoria, with SASSA and NSFAS disbursement integrations in progress.

  • Double-entry ledger. A zero-sum invariant enforced on every transaction, plus an automated reconciliation bridge from the database into the Sage general ledger.
  • Multi-party fee engine. Platform, gateway and merchant commission settled to retailers through Paystack Split Payments, so the platform never holds merchant funds.
  • Bank rails. Defined the Capitec integration (Connect login, DebiCheck, inherited FICA/KYC) and led PASA audit readiness.

How I work

  1. Step 01

    Design the flow

    Service design, research and prototypes with the people who will use the product, before any production code.

  2. Step 02

    Build the backend

    Schemas, ledgers and invariants come first. TypeScript and PostgreSQL, event-driven where the data moves.

  3. Step 03

    Ship the app

    I ship daily with Claude Code and Cursor. Every component and migration is reviewed against the design system, accessibility rules and data model before it merges.

  4. Step 04

    Own it in production

    Monitoring, alerts, OTA rollouts and incident response. I stay responsible for what I ship.
