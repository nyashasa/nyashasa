How I work

  1. Step 01

    Find the opportunity

    Market analysis, customer interviews and a business model, tested before anything is built.

  2. Step 02

    Shape the product

    Service design and prototypes tested with real users, with pricing and unit economics worked out alongside.

  3. Step 03

    Build the team and platform

    The right people and partners, plus architecture and review standards that let a lean team ship quickly with AI coding agents.

    Grow with partners

    Pilots with operators, retailers and banks. We measure what works, then scale it together.
